ANNUAL REVIEW

Welcome to our annual review. This document describes our people, our network,
our approach to risk and our results. It has been prepared for a fictional
retrieval exercise and contains no real company information.

Contents: chairman's letter; who we are; our network; our people; customers and
service; market context; risk management; treasury and funding; accounting
policies; results for the period; governance; sustainability.

The review uses plain-English headings. Readers looking for specific figures
should refer to the results section.

CHAIRMAN'S LETTER

It has been a demanding but rewarding period. Our teams handled record volumes
through the winter peak, and we opened two new sites in the north. I want to
thank every colleague for their commitment.

The board met regularly throughout the fiscal year to review strategy, safety
and capital allocation. We welcomed two new non-executive directors, and the
audit committee completed a full review of internal controls.

Looking ahead, we remain focused on disciplined growth, service quality and
the safety of our people. The board believes the group is well placed for the
coming fiscal year, although conditions in the wider economy remain uncertain.

Our annual general meeting will be held in September. Shareholders will be
invited to approve the directors' remuneration report and the reappointment
of the auditors.

WHO WE ARE

The registered entity is Harbourline Logistics Group plc, a holding company
whose subsidiaries trade under the Harbourline name.

We operate a network of temperature-controlled warehouses and run refrigerated
road haulage and freight forwarding for food producers and retailers. Our
facilities store chilled and frozen goods, and our vehicles move them from
ports and farms to distribution centres.

Founded as a single cold store beside a harbour, we have grown steadily
through new sites and selective acquisitions. Our purpose is simple: keep
food safe and fresh from the dock to the shelf.

OUR NETWORK

We now run nineteen sites with a combined storage capacity of over 400,000
pallet positions. Seven sites are near major ports, and the rest serve inland
distribution hubs.

The fleet comprises around 600 refrigerated trailers and 350 tractor units.
Telematics on every vehicle report temperature, location and fuel use in real
time, and our control tower monitors every journey around the clock.

During the year we invested in automated racking at two sites and installed
rooftop solar panels at five warehouses. We also extended our cross-docking
operation, which lets goods move from inbound to outbound vehicles without
long-term storage.

OUR PEOPLE

We employ approximately 4,200 colleagues, including drivers, warehouse
operatives, engineers and office teams. Staff turnover among drivers fell
during the year after we introduced new shift patterns and a retention bonus.
Overall staff turnover remains higher than we would like in warehouse roles,
and reducing it is a priority.

Safety is our first value. The lost-time injury rate improved for the third
consecutive year. Every new colleague completes a safety induction, and
refresher training is mandatory each year.

We continued to invest in apprenticeships and driver academies, training 180
new drivers. Employee engagement scores rose, and colleagues told us they
value flexible scheduling and clear career paths.

CUSTOMERS AND SERVICE

We serve food manufacturers, importers, wholesalers and retailers. Customer
satisfaction scores improved, and on-time delivery reached 98.6%. Our customer
service team handles queries around the clock, and customer complaints fell.

Our biggest client, a national supermarket chain, accounted for 27% of
turnover in the year. Its current contract runs until 2028.

Our sales team won new contracts with three frozen food importers and a
plant-based dairy brand. Sales pipeline activity remains healthy, although
some customers delayed decisions because of economic uncertainty.

MARKET CONTEXT

The cold-chain logistics market continues to expand as consumers buy more
chilled and frozen food and as retailers outsource storage and transport.
Industry analysts expect market growth of around 4% per year over the medium
term. Growth in online grocery shopping is driving demand for smaller,
faster deliveries.

Competition remains intense. Larger competitors are investing in automation,
and smaller regional operators compete on price. We believe our port-centric
network, strong safety record and service quality differentiate us.

Energy prices, driver availability and regulation on refrigerant gases are
the main external factors affecting the industry.

RISK MANAGEMENT

The board sets the group's risk appetite, and the audit committee oversees
the risk register. Principal risks include loss of a major customer, energy
cost increases, driver shortages, cyber attacks, food safety incidents and
currency movements.

Currency risk: some equipment and refrigerant gases are purchased from
overseas suppliers. Our currency hedging policy allows forward contracts for
up to twelve months of committed foreign currency purchases. We do not
speculate on currency movements.

Interest rate risk is managed by fixing a portion of borrowing costs. Credit
risk is managed through credit limits and regular customer reviews.

TREASURY AND FUNDING

Our treasury team manages cash, liquidity and bank relationships. The group
has a committed revolving credit facility and a term loan with a syndicate of
four banks, both maturing in 2029. Covenants were met with comfortable
headroom throughout the year.

Cash management: surplus cash is held in instant-access deposits with
highly rated banks. We aim to keep a strong balance sheet that can fund
growth while maintaining investment-grade style ratios.

At the year end, borrowings less cash and equivalents stood at 48.0 million
pounds, compared with 52.5 million pounds a year earlier.

ACCOUNTING POLICIES

Basis of preparation: these fictional statements cover the twelve months
ended 31 March 2026 and are presented in pounds sterling, rounded to the
nearest hundred thousand.

Depreciation is charged on a straight-line basis over the useful life of
each asset: buildings over 40 years, racking over 15 years, refrigeration
plant over 12 years and vehicles over 7 years. Depreciation starts when an
asset is brought into use. Amortisation of acquired customer relationships
is charged over 10 years. Depreciation and amortization methods are reviewed
each year.

Revenue recognition: storage income is recognised over the period goods are
held; transport income is recognised when delivery is complete.

RESULTS FOR THE PERIOD

Turnover for the twelve months was 212.4 million pounds, an increase of 9%
compared with the previous twelve months, helped by new contracts and higher
storage occupancy.

Operating profit before depreciation and amortisation was 31.8 million
pounds, reflecting higher volumes partly offset by energy and wage costs.

Operating profit after these charges was 17.2 million pounds. Capital
expenditure of 24.6 million pounds was focused on automation, solar panels
and fleet renewal.

GOVERNANCE

The board comprises a non-executive chair, three executive directors and
four independent non-executive directors. The audit, remuneration and
nomination committees each met at least three times during the fiscal year.

The auditors' report on these fictional statements was unqualified. The
board reviewed the effectiveness of risk management and internal control
and found no significant weaknesses.

Directors' remuneration is linked to safety, service quality, cash
generation and long-term shareholder returns.

SUSTAINABILITY

We aim to reach net zero across our own operations by 2040. This year we cut
emissions per pallet stored by 6% through solar power, LED lighting and more
efficient refrigeration. Twenty electric tractor units joined the fleet for
urban deliveries.

We reduced food waste by redistributing surplus stock to charities with our
customers' agreement. Water use per site fell, and all new buildings meet
high environmental standards.

We report against recognised climate disclosure frameworks and publish our
full sustainability data online.
