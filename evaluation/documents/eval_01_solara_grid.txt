ANNUAL REVIEW — SOLARA GRID PLC

FICTIONAL EXAMPLE FOR EVALUATION — NOT A REAL COMPANY.

This review describes our assets, people, risks and results.
OUTLOOK

We target an EBITDA margin of 61% by 2027 as new assets reach full operation.

Our pipeline of committed projects underpins growth, although timing depends on permits and grid connections.
MARKET CONTEXT

Demand for essential infrastructure remains resilient. Industry analysts expect market growth of around 5% per year over the medium term, supported by population growth and decarbonisation policy.

Competition for high-quality assets is intense, and inflation-linked contracts provide some protection against rising costs.
OUR PEOPLE

We employ around 1,800 colleagues across operations, engineering and support functions. Staff turnover fell during the year after we introduced new shift patterns and a retention programme.

Safety remains our first priority, and the lost-time injury rate improved again. Employee engagement scores rose for the third consecutive year.
TREASURY AND FUNDING

The group is funded through long-dated bonds and a committed revolving credit facility. Gross borrowings at the year end were 871.3 million pounds sterling, with an average maturity of nine years.

Covenants were met with comfortable headroom. Surplus cash is held in instant-access deposits with highly rated banks.
RESULTS FOR THE PERIOD

Turnover for the period was 769.1 million pounds sterling. This was an increase of 17% on the prior year.

Operating profit before depreciation and amortisation was 407.6 million pounds sterling. Adjusted EBITDA, which excludes one-off restructuring costs, was 411.4 million pounds sterling.

Capital expenditure focused on new assets and maintenance.
SUSTAINABILITY

We aim to reach net zero across our own operations by 2040. Emissions intensity fell this year as we switched to renewable electricity at all major sites.

We publish our full climate disclosures online and report against recognised frameworks.
CUSTOMERS AND CONTRACTS

Our largest customer accounted for 12% of revenue.

Contract renewals are staggered, and counterparties are regularly reviewed for credit quality.
GOVERNANCE

The board comprises a non-executive chair, two executive directors and five independent non-executive directors. Each committee met at least four times during the fiscal year.

Directors' remuneration is linked to safety, availability, cash generation and long-term returns.
BASIS OF PREPARATION

This fictional review has been prepared for a document retrieval exercise.

All amounts in this review are presented in pounds sterling.

These results cover the year ended 31 December 2024.
FIVE-YEAR PERSPECTIVE

In the prior year, turnover was 657.4 million pounds sterling. Over five years the asset base has more than doubled through construction and selective acquisitions.

We have increased our dividend every year since listing.
FINANCIAL POSITION

Borrowings less cash and cash equivalents stood at 791.2 million pounds sterling at 31 December 2024.

Liquidity remains strong, and no major refinancing is due within two years.
WHO WE ARE

Solara Grid plc (the Company) heads the group described in this review.

The group develops and operates utility-scale solar farms that sell electricity under long-term power purchase agreements.

Our assets are long-lived and most of our income is contracted or regulated.
CHAIRMAN'S LETTER

It has been a year of steady progress for Solara Grid. The board met regularly throughout the fiscal year to review strategy, safety and capital allocation.

We welcomed two new independent directors, and the audit committee completed a full review of internal controls. The board expects the coming fiscal year to bring further investment.
